About

Originally, the Chernoff Faces were a data scientist collective. Today, the Chernoff Faces consist primarily of artists and designers.

Not a big one. Some of us work in the field or are at least interested in it.

Chernoff faces are a lovely multivariate method for intuitively visualizing data in the form of human-like faces. They were introduced by the physicist Herman Chernoff. We liked the name. There were no more serious reasons than that.

We considered the visual arts — the field most of us came from — to be completely dead. A gigantic, glittering, hedonistic nonstop party, organized by and for those happy few who are part of the nepotistic system they help stabilize and from which they profit. So we simply fled to the nearest field, whose conditions seemed more conducive to cultural production.

Because we can bring our ideas for a better world (in the broadest sense) to life with very little financial or energy investment. Thanks to our digital tools, we can operate at extremely low production costs and are not dependent on the goodwill of sponsors, let alone the willingness of grant providers or producers to collaborate. We can therefore offer cultural productions at minimal cost that meet the highest standards.
