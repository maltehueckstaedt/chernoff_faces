About

The Chernoff Faces are an association consisting of contemporary artists and designers. The group originally originated as a collective of data scientists who founded the project as a recreational activity. In scientific terms, Chernoff Faces refer to a methodology developed by Herman Chernoff in 1973 to visualize multivariate data through distinct human facial features.

Data science plays no role in the artistic production of the Chernoff Faces. The conceptual framework of the work is instead influenced by perspectives and principles derived from the field of sociology.
